# 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Darpan Labs - 18 Digital Twin Personas

## Overview
These 18 distinct e-commerce personas represent the full spectrum of online shopping behaviors, from budget-conscious deal seekers to premium quality enthusiasts. Each persona is powered by comprehensive behavioral, psychographic, and demographic profiles.

## OCEAN Personality Model

Each persona includes an OCEAN (Big Five) personality profile:

- **O (Openness):** Curiosity, creativity, preference for novelty
- **C (Conscientiousness):** Organization, dependability, self-discipline
- **E (Extraversion):** Sociability, assertiveness, emotional expressiveness
- **A (Agreeableness):** Compassion, cooperativeness, trust
- **N (Neuroticism):** Emotional stability, anxiety, moodiness

Scores range from 0.0 (low) to 1.0 (high).
